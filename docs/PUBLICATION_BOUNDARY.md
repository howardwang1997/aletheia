# Evidence and publication boundary

Public scientific claims require analyses that implement the current valid protocol. Development,
post-hoc and confirmatory analyses retain their respective labels and authority limits.
Engineering qualification receipts establish only the execution scope stated in their contracts.

Internal implementation defects, debugging, invalid endpoints, operator errors and development
iterations are not scientific findings. They cannot support public research background, motivation,
novelty, evidence, results, limitations, figures or claims. Incident histories are retained only as
explicitly marked private/internal audit records.

Local private records live under `workspaces/private/internal/`, outside version control. The
repository excludes workspace, artifact and internal-document trees from source exports by default.
Do not copy them into README, release notes, papers or supplementary materials. A public artifact
with incident-derived scientific framing must be corrected before publication.

Operator guides describe current contracts, procedures and qualification status. They do not
promote local tests, historical deployment work or partial campaigns into scientific evidence.
